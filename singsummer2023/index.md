*July 27, 2023, 10am--5:40pm*

*Location:* NUS AS8 04-01

The Singapore Summer Meeting is an informal meeting of formal linguists currently and formerly based in Singapore to share their work in progress.

<!-- ![Singapore Summer Meeting 2023](photo.jpg) -->

*Schedule*

- 10am: intro
- 10:10--noon:
	- Myunghye Yoo and Zheng Shen: Conjunction as Attractor
	- Zheng Shen and Myunghye Yoo: Processing Closest Agreement with Disjunction Subjects
	- Serene Siow and Nick Huang: Seeing is believing? Polysemy of perception verbs for belief
- noon--1pm: lunch (provided)
- 1--2:45pm:
	- JJ Lim (UCSD): PPCs in two varieties of Mongolian
	- Beth Chan and Zheng Shen: Conditions on wh-the-hell licensing: Evidence from Colloquial Singapore English
	- Tham Shiao Wei : Aspectual marking in Mandarin Chinese on a scale of attainment
- 2:45pm: break
- 3pm:
	- Tamisha Tan (NTU): Stative nouns without stative verbs: Overt existential closure in Amarasi in ma-constructions
	- Henrison Hsieh (HK PolyU) and Michael Yoshitaka Erlewine: On the scope and position of Tagalog clitic adverbs
- 4:15pm: break
- 4:30pm:
	- Alex Smith: History and syntax of the languages of Borneo
	- Michael Yoshitaka Erlewine: Learning the organization of the verbal phase in Philippine-type and Indonesian-type languages

The meeting is open and free for all. Organized by Zheng and mitcho. Supported by NUS.
